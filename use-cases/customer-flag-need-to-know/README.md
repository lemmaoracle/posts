# Use Case: Need-to-Know Field Signals

## Thesis

**The mark travels. The reason doesn't.**

High-risk-customer handling, credit flags, two-person-rule triggers — to act, the front line needs only the fact that a case *applies* (the mark), not the reason behind it. Show the reason and you invite leakage and inconsistent judgment; hide it and you can't later prove the flag was legitimate. Lemma circulates only the **mark (handling category)** to the front line as a proof, while the underlying basis stays with the back office (authorized staff) and is viewed via selective disclosure. Who set the mark, when, and on what basis remains tamper-proof as **provenance (Pillar 01)**, so audits, complaints, and subject-access requests can be answered by proving the issuing basis after the fact — only as needed. Keep your existing customer management and blacklist sharing; add only the proof layer.
